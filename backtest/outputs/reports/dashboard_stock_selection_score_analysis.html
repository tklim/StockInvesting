Promotion rule

A replacement requires at least ten validated independent one-year out-of-sample cohorts, positive median excess versus SPY and QQQ, better median Sortino than baseline, maximum drawdown no more than two percentage points worse, and a positive lower bootstrap bound for improvement. Otherwise the production 25/25/25/15/10 score remains.

Research support only. No fundamentals, valuation, taxes, position sizing, or trade execution are included.
